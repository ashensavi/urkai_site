FAQ

Useful answers. No gym-bro mythology.

What kind of equipment does URKAI offer?+

URKAI’s range is structured around strength equipment, racks and benches, cardio products and training accessories. Exact products will be added as the catalogue is finalized.

Is URKAI suitable for home gyms and commercial spaces?+

The range is intended to cover both use cases. Each product page can clearly state its recommended setting, dimensions and intended load so buyers can choose appropriately.

Where can I find product specifications?+

Specifications will appear on each product card and detailed product page once the final catalogue, measurements and warranty information are supplied.

Do products include a warranty?+

Warranty terms should be shown per product rather than making one blanket promise. That keeps the information accurate and avoids the ancient retail tradition of discovering exclusions only after something breaks.

Can I get help choosing equipment for a full gym setup?+

Yes. A consultation or quotation section can be connected to your sales team once you provide the preferred WhatsApp number, phone number or enquiry form destination.
